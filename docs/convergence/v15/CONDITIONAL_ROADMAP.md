# V15 CONDITIONAL CAPABILITY ROADMAP & BOUNDARY GOVERNANCE

## Scope
Defines the conditional execution boundaries for P2 and P3 capabilities as mandated by Section 05 (WP11 & WP12).

### 1. Conditional Growth Capabilities (P2 / P3)
- **F36: Trip-Length Optimizer**: Prerequisite: Authoritative departure/return pair matrix across 21-day window.
- **F37: Weekend Escape Finder**: Prerequisite: Local timezone departure/arrival schedule availability.
- **F38: Holiday Discovery Calendar**: Prerequisite: Official holiday dataset by country.
- **F39: Nearby Airport Economics**: Prerequisite: Ground transfer cost data source.
- **F40: Itinerary Quality Score**: Prerequisite: Layover duration and self-transfer segment tagging.
- **F43: Weekly Deal Digest**: Prerequisite: Active user opt-in and consent database.
- **F44: Rule-Based Personalized Feed**: Prerequisite: User home airport preference profiles.
- **F46: Dynamic Editorial Collections**: Prerequisite: Saved query templates in Postgres.
- **F47: Provider Trust Metric**: Prerequisite: Accumulated multi-week error rate telemetry.
- **F48: Adaptive Scan Prioritization**: Prerequisite: Operational cost model per route.
- **F86: Multi-City & Open-Jaw Routing**: Prerequisite: Multi-leg graph solver and provider support.
- **F87: Collaborative Travel Planning**: Prerequisite: Multi-user shared session permissions.
- **F88: Travel Readiness & Entry Feed**: Prerequisite: Authoritative government entry requirement API.

### 2. High-Risk Commercial Extensions (P3 Boundary Locked)
- **F49: AI Travel Assistant Copilot**: Held pending user safety boundary verification.
- **F50: Predictive Buy/Wait Forecasting**: Strictly held pending out-of-sample calibration.
- **F51: Premium Paid Watch Subscriptions**: Held pending payment gateway licensing.
- **F52: Direct Booking Checkout Engine**: Held pending IATA / airline ticketing merchant rights.
- **F53: Commercial B2B Opportunity API**: Held pending data redistribution contracts.
- **F54: Credit Card & Promotion Engine**: Held pending bank affiliate agreements.
- **F55: International Market Expansion**: Held pending localized currency settlement.
